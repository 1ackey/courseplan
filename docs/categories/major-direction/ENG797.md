## 考核方式

### Assessment

- **Test and assignment: 30%**
- **Reading notes: 10%**
  - Electronic version of your notes.
  - Critiques, questions, and reflections are welcome.
- **Final: 60%**

### Test (T)

The test includes:

- Multiple-choice questions.
- Fill-in-the-blank questions.

### Assignment (A)

A **3-minute video** introducing the distinction between either:

1. **Phenotype vs. cryptotype**, or
2. **Enation vs. agnation**

The video must include:

- Definitions of the concepts.
- Examples to help viewers understand the concepts.

### Final Exam

You may bring **one A4 page** of notes.

The final exam includes:

- Multiple-choice questions.
- Fill-in-the-blank questions.
- Explanation of key concepts with examples.
- Syntactic analyses.
